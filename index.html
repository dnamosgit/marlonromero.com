About the artist

Nature, spirit, and memory

Marlon Romero Gomez was born in Pucallpa, Peru, a city beside the Amazon rainforest — the place his work keeps returning to.

Both self-taught and trained, he developed as a visionary and environmental artist, painting primarily in oil and acrylic. The rainforest is his constant subject: its landscapes, and the life they hold.

Alongside the forest, his paintings explore the indigenous and mestizo religious belief systems, the spirituality, and the culture of Peru — a meeting of nature, imagination, and the human experience.

His work has been presented in museums, galleries, conservation programs, and international exhibitions, and his paintings have been featured and sold internationally.
